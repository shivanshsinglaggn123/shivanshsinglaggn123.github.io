This site documents my high school research on the chromatography of homopolymers and polymers with functional end groups. I am working with Dr. Yongmei Wang from the University of Memphis.

My current work combines the equations from the research papers with Python and Jupyter notebooks. I calculate partition coefficients, generate graphs, investigate how model parameters change the results, and record the research process in blog posts.

The two main project areas are the [Homopolymer Graph](/Homopolymer%20Graph/) and [End-functionalized Plots](/End-functionalized%20Plots/).
