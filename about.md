I am a junior at Houston High School and a student researcher working with Dr. Yongmei Wang from the University of Memphis.

This research investigates the partition coefficients of homopolymers and end-functionalized polymers in chromatographic systems. I am using mathematical equations, Python, and Jupyter notebooks to study how polymer size, chain length, pore size, and functional end groups affect polymer behavior.

My goal is to understand the relationship between polymer structure and chromatography, develop reproducible calculations, and communicate the research process clearly through this blog.
